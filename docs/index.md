# Strengthening Indigenous Seed Conservation Systems in Kenya

### Reclaim Our Seed, Reclaim Our Future

**Welcome to Mbegu Link. Conserving our future depends on how we conserve and protect our seeds.**

Mbegu Link serves as a community-controlled digital information bridge designed to preserve seed identity, source history, viability data, and formal custody links as seed lots migrate between smallholder systems and institutional genebanks.
